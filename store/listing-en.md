# Play Store listing — English

**App name** (up to 30 characters)

Chamas de Vardren

**Short description** (up to 80 characters)

Turn-based strategy: found cities, negotiate treaties and conquer the world.

**Full description** (up to 4000 characters)

Nine tribes fight over a world covered in ash. In Chamas de Vardren you start with a single village and decide, turn by turn, how it becomes a kingdom: expanding into new lands, researching technologies, negotiating with your neighbors or marching against them.

GROWING CITIES
• Harvest resources, build farms, mines, sawmills and markets and watch your cities level up.
• Specialize each city: military, science, trade, farming or port, each with its own buildings and milestones.
• Invest in growth, hold festivals and patronage, and decide what fits in your building slots.

WAR WITH STRATEGY
• Over 20 unit types on land and sea, with flanking, formations, high ground and ambushes.
• Besiege cities, cut enemy supply lines, face rebels and partisans.
• Generals, fortifications, spies and special abilities.

DIPLOMACY AND GOVERNMENT
• Peace, pacts, alliances, trade, tribute and joint wars. Every tribe remembers what you did.
• Demand reparations, cities or vassalage to sign peace.
• Choose your form of government, set taxes and take loans.
• Whoever pulls too far ahead faces a coalition.

A NEW WORLD EVERY GAME
• Maps from 14×14 to 40×40: pangaea, continents, archipelago or lakes.
• Six victory types: domination, science, economy, wonders, territory and diplomacy.
• Scenarios, world events, ruins, wonders and achievements.
• Four AI difficulty levels.

MADE FOR PHONES
• Plays offline. Games are saved on your device.
• Several players on the same device (pass the phone).
• Portuguese, English and Spanish.
• Original music and sound effects.

TRIBES
Six free tribes (Aymará, Tupinás, Vikar, Qadir, Han-Lu and Zambé) and three store tribes (Kemet, Tarkhan and Ériu), each with its own playstyle. Store tribes are not stronger, just different, and they stay on your Google Play account.

The game is free, with ads only between games and an optional video that gives stars. "No ads" and the extra tribes are optional purchases.
